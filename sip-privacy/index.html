Sip Privacy

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 July 2026

Sip is a personal wine journal and taste profiling app created by Tristan Thomas. This policy explains what information the app processes and how it is used.

Information you provide in the app

Sip stores the wines, ratings, notes and other details you add to your journal, together with the taste profile it creates for you.

If you use the camera or photo library, Sip processes the bottle or wine-list images you choose so it can identify wines and fill in their details.

How your data is used

Sip uses your data to:

  • Save and display your wine journal entries.
  • Generate your taste profile and tasting summaries.
  • Let you search across wines you have rated.
  • Identify wines from barcodes, bottle photos and wine-list photos when you use those features.
  • Look up wine information online when you use search, Predict and enrichment features.

Improving taste predictions

Sip sends your wine ratings, rating dates, the predictions shown at the time and useful wine details such as names, producers, vintages, regions, grapes and characteristics. This helps test and improve Sip's taste predictions.

Ratings are grouped using a separate identifier so they can be evaluated together. Sip does not attach your name, email, notes, photos, price or your location. Deleting a wine from your journal also tells the service to remove its contributed rating.

Contributed ratings are stored with Cloudflare. A contribution set is removed after two years without an update.

Online search and enrichment

When you use online search, prediction or enrichment, Sip sends only the query, barcode, wine details or image needed to provide that feature. Cloudflare and the relevant search or AI provider process the request and return the result.

Storage and syncing

Your journal is stored on your device. If iCloud syncing is available and enabled, it is also stored in your iCloud account so it stays in sync across your devices.

Accounts

Sip does not require you to create an account.

Data sharing

Sip does not sell your data. It is shared only with the service providers needed to run Sip's online features and with Apple when you use iCloud.

Contact

If you have privacy questions about Sip, contact t@tristanthomas.me.
