Sip

Figure out your wine taste.

Sip is a personal wine journal and taste-profile app for people who want clearer answers about what they actually enjoy drinking.

What Sip does

Rate wines, keep notes, and build a profile from the bottles you actually drink. Sip also includes Predict, so you can search for a wine, scan a barcode, photograph a bottle, or scan a restaurant wine list and get a quick estimate of how well it matches your taste.
